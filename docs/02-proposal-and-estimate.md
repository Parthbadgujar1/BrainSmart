## 1. Understanding of the project

BrainSmart School is newly opened and needs a professional, modern website to attract and inform families. The client asked for:

- **The same pages and structure** as `https://centervillees.gcpsk12.org/`, but "more perfect and modern".
- Delivery in **6 weeks**.

## 2. What we will deliver

A fast, mobile-first, accessible single-page-application website built with **React**, hosted on **Vercel**:

| Area | Included |
|---|---|
| **Structure** | All pages mirrored from the reference site — 6 main menu sections, 3 levels of navigation, 40+ inner pages (see `03-sitemap-and-scope.md`) |
| **Home page** | Slideshow hero (pause/play, keyboard accessible), 7 quick links, welcome/pillars section, family technology help banner, portal tiles, news, upcoming events |
| **Inner pages** | Breadcrumbs, "In This Section" sidebar, consistent template, easy-to-edit content |
| **Calendar** | Month / Week / Day views, printable, driven by one data file |
| **Faculty & Staff directory** | Search by keyword, first name, last name; alphabetical |
| **News & Announcements** | List + individual story pages |
| **Site search** | Searches all pages, news and staff |
| **Forms** | Contact + admissions enquiry (email delivery, spam protection) |
| **Utility bar** | Student & Family portal links, search, social links |
| **Footer** | Address, phones, social, legal/accessibility/nondiscrimination links, site map |
| **Design** | Modern, branded to the school's logo and colours; responsive for phones, tablets, desktops |
| **Quality** | Accessibility best practice (skip link, keyboard nav, ARIA, contrast), SEO basics, auto-generated `sitemap.xml`, performance tuning |
| **Launch** | Vercel deployment, domain connection, SSL, redirect `non-www → www` |
| **Handover** | Source code, documentation, 1-hour staff training session, 30 days post-launch support |

**Out of scope** (available as add-ons): custom CMS/admin panel, online payments, student/parent login portals, photo/video production, content writing, translation of all content, logo design.

## 6. Domain — `.edu` is not available

The requested `https://www.brainsmartschool.edu` **cannot be registered** for a K-12 school: the `.edu` top-level domain is restricted to US post-secondary institutions accredited by a recognised accrediting agency (managed by EDUCAUSE). Recommended alternatives:

1. **`brainsmartschool.org`** — recommended; widely used by schools and non-profits. Site served at `www.brainsmartschool.org`.
2. `brainsmartschool.school` — descriptive and modern.
3. `brainsmartschool.com` — most familiar to the public.

Full details: `04-domain-hosting.md`. We register the domain after the client picks one (availability confirmed at that time).
